## 1. Users — Talk Before You Build More
Missing: proof that students will use this weekly.
- Interview 10 students from 1 target college. Ask: how they find PYQs today, what fails, what they would pay or share for.
- Pick 1 pilot college, 1 branch, 1 semester. Launch there first, not all universities.
- Simple waitlist with college email. Goal: 100 signups before Phase 1 ends.
- Links to: PRD-MVP.md scope, ROADMAP.md Phase 1.

## 2. Content Supply — Library Will Be Empty on Day 1
Missing: who provides PYQs and checks accuracy.
- Decide: start with contributor uploads plus reviewer approval already approved in ARCHITECTURE-PLAN.md, or seed with public syllabus links only.
- Copyright rule: only uploads you have rights to share. Takedown contact from day one.
- Incentive: top contributors get early access or recognition. No cash promise at start.
- Quality bar: wrong PYQ or wrong answer explanation kills trust. Reviewer checklist required before Approve.

## 3. Money — Costs and Pricing
Missing: monthly cost ceiling and freemium line.
- Cost heads: Supabase project, file storage 100 MB starter per student, email and push provider, domain, AI orchestration with starter 5 notes, 3 tests, 5 summaries per day. See SUPABASE-PLAN.md Sec 10 and Section 10 below.
- Starter locked: free limits above, paid tier open. Do not promise unlimited AI.
- Runway: keep MVP to Phase 0 plus Phase 1 only. Phase 2 and 3 cost extra.

## 4. Legal Beyond DPDP — Still Needed

## 5. Launch — One College, Not All India
Missing: go-to-market slice.
- Launch order: 1 college → attendance plus timetable daily habit → PYQs plus tests weekly habit → flashcards retention → then career and community in Phase 2 and 3.
- Referral: invite classmates for shared decks and teams. No paid ads at start.
- Support: one owner for grievance redressal with 90-day cap already in PRD-MVP.md. Publish contact in app.

## 6. Metrics — Know If It Works
Missing: 5 numbers to track weekly.
- Activation: signup to dashboard with subjects set.
- Weekly active: timetable check plus one test or review.
- Retention: week 1 to week 4 return.
- Content health: pending to approved time, rejection rate.
- Trust: correction reports, grievance response time.
- No vanity metrics: downloads alone mean nothing.

## 7. Team and Ops — Who Does What
Missing: named owners.
- Builder, reviewer for content, support and grievance owner, incident plus Board reporting owners from COMPLIANCE-DPDP.md Sec 6, access review owner.
- If solo: you own all five until first teammate. Document handover points.

## 8. Risks for Students
- Wrong answers in tests and notes. Mitigation: source links, reviewer approval, report wrong content button in Phase 1.
- Attendance disputes if self-marked. Keep default self-marked per ROADMAP.md, show history for faculty check.
- Under-18 accounts. Keep age gate plus parental consent per COMPLIANCE-DPDP.md Sec 8.
- Community abuse. Keep community in Phase 3 only after moderation spec already approved.
