5. Timetable & Student Attendance Tracking
5.1 Timetable Management
Weekly Timetable Setup — Enter or upload the regular weekly timetable.
Day-Wise Timetable — View classes scheduled for each day.
Period-Wise Timetable — Display individual periods with their subjects and timings.
Subject and Classroom Details — Store subject names, faculty display names, and classroom information. Faculty names are display-only, no staff login.
Semester-Based Timetable — Maintain a timetable for the current semester.
Timetable Editing — Add, edit, or remove regular timetable periods.
Timetable Updates — Update the recurring timetable when the semester schedule changes.
5.2 One-Day Timetable Changes
Date-Specific Timetable Editing — Change the schedule for one selected date without affecting the regular weekly timetable.
One-Day Subject Changes — Replace a scheduled subject for a particular day.
One-Day Period Changes — Change a period's timing or position for a particular date.
Extra Classes — Add classes scheduled outside the regular timetable.
Cancelled Classes — Mark classes that will not take place.
Rescheduled Classes — Move a class to another period or time for a specific day.
Classroom Changes — Update the classroom for a particular class.
Holiday Management — Mark holidays when regular classes do not take place.
Restore Regular Timetable — Return to the recurring schedule after a date-specific exception.
5.3 Attendance Management
Self-Marked Attendance Only — No Faculty Verification, No College Staff Login
Quick Attendance Marking — Mark each class as Present, Absent, or Unmarked.
Period-Wise Attendance — Record attendance for individual periods.
Subject-Wise Attendance — Calculate attendance separately for each subject.
Overall Attendance Percentage — Display the student's overall attendance.
Attendance History — View attendance records by date, period, and subject.
Attendance Target Setting — Set a desired attendance percentage.
Low-Attendance Alerts — Highlight subjects below the target.
Attendance Recovery Estimate — Estimate how many future classes must be attended to reach the target.
Automatic Attendance Recalculation — Update attendance when classes or attendance records change.
Attendance Dashboard — Show attendance totals, percentages, missed classes, and upcoming classes.
Important rule: Cancelled classes and holidays should not count as absences. Date-specific timetable changes should affect only the selected date unless the student explicitly updates the regular timetable.
